CafeKiosk Payment Amount Recording - Reliable UI Fix

- Staff POS and Manager POS now use ONE visible numeric payment field.
- Cash: label is "Cash Received" and Change is calculated.
- GCash/Online: label becomes "Amount Paid Online / GCash".
- Card: label becomes "Amount Paid by Card".
- The exact tendered amount is persisted as orders.payment_amount.
- cash_received is stored only for Cash payments.
- The POS script is cache-busted with ?v=payment-amount-v2 so Railway/browser cache cannot keep the old hidden-field behavior.
- Backend still validates that the amount paid covers the final total.
