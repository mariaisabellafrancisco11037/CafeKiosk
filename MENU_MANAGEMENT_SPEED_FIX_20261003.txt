Menu Management speed fix - 2026-10-03

- Renders cafe-scoped cached menu immediately.
- Uses a compact catalog response that does not embed every base64 product image in JSON.
- Product images are served individually by an authenticated tenant-scoped image endpoint.
- Product images use browser lazy loading and asynchronous decoding.
- Availability and ingredient suggestion requests load after the catalog is already usable.
- No menu/product records are removed or reseeded by this change.
