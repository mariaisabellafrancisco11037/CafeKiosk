CafeKiosk Custom Staff / Manager Invitation Email
==================================================

The invitation email was updated to use the cafe's registered name dynamically.

Example subject:
You're invited to join Bean & Brew Cafe as Staff

Example message:
Hello!

You've been invited by the owner of Bean & Brew Cafe to join their Bean & Brew Cafe team as Staff.

[Accept Staff Invitation]

This invitation was sent to you because the administrator of Bean & Brew Cafe added you as a Staff member.

If you weren't expecting this invitation, you can safely ignore this email.

Thank you,
Bean & Brew Cafe
Powered by CafeKiosk

The same template automatically changes Staff to Manager for manager invitations.
The invitation remains secure, expiring, and single-use.

Email template file:
CafeKiosk-Backend/services/emailService.js
