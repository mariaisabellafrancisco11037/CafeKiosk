CafeKiosk Employee Archive + Manager Sidebar Update

1. Admin > User
- Active/current employees stay in the main User Accounts table.
- Fire & Archive signs the employee out immediately and moves the account to Former Employee Archives.
- The archive shows the last archive reason, archive date, and administrator who archived the account.
- Restore returns the former employee account to the current employee list and enables login again.
- Account-status history and Audit Logs are preserved in MySQL.

2. Manager sidebar
- Sidebar navigation is icon-only.
- The only sidebar text kept is the registered cafe name.
- Dashboard, POS, and Order Queue remain available through icons with tooltips/accessibility labels.
- Applied to Manager Dashboard, Manager POS, and Manager Order Queue.
