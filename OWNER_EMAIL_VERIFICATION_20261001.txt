CafeKiosk Owner Email Verification

New owner signup flow:
1. Owner registers with their own email address.
2. Account/cafe remain Pending and Inactive.
3. CafeKiosk sends a Resend verification email to that exact registered owner email.
4. Verification link is single-use and expires after 30 minutes.
5. Clicking Verify & Activate validates the hashed token, marks the email verified, activates the owner, and approves/activates the cafe.
6. The owner is then shown a Sign in to CafeKiosk button.

Required Railway variables:
RESEND_API_KEY=<private key>
RESEND_FROM_EMAIL=invites@cafekiosk.site
RESEND_FROM_NAME=CafeKiosk
APP_URL=https://cafekiosk.site

Existing SMTP_* Gmail settings are not changed by this feature.
Owner verification explicitly uses Resend.
