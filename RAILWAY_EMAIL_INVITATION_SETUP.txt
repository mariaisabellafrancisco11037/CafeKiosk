CafeKiosk Railway Email Invitations
====================================

CafeKiosk now uses Resend HTTPS API for Staff / Manager invitation emails.
SMTP/Gmail settings are no longer required for this feature.

See:
  RESEND_EMAIL_INVITATION_SETUP.txt

Required Railway variables:
  RESEND_API_KEY
  RESEND_FROM_EMAIL

Optional:
  RESEND_FROM_NAME=CafeKiosk

The employee recipient email is entered by the cafe owner in Admin > Users.
The cafe name and Staff/Manager role are generated dynamically in each invitation.
