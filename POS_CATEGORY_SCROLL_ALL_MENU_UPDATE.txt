CafeKiosk POS Category Navigation Update

- Staff POS and Manager POS category navigation now scrolls horizontally on the X axis.
- A visible horizontal scrollbar is provided when categories exceed the available width.
- Mouse wheel / trackpad vertical scrolling over the category bar also moves it horizontally.
- "All Menu Items" is always the first category and displays products from every active category.
- Dynamic categories created by the Admin continue to appear after All Menu Items.
- Selecting a category scrolls its tab into view.
