CafeKiosk fix - 2026-10-03

1. Same-browser Admin / Manager / Staff role isolation
- Role-specific HttpOnly cookies remain separate.
- X-Cafe-Role now selects ONLY the matching cookie; it no longer falls through to another logged-in role.
- Each tab verifies that /api/auth/me returns exactly the active role.
- Logging out one role does not clear the other role cookies/sessions.

2. Menu Management Product Catalog slider restored
- Product Catalog has its own visible vertical scrollbar again.
- Whole-page scrolling is retained so the thesis footer remains reachable.
- Menu products/data are not modified by this change.
